Frequently Asked Questions

Answers before the first project conversation

Each answer appears as visible page content and matching structured data.

Does Elettro provide complete website projects?

Yes. Elettro handles discovery, information architecture, design, development, content structure, SEO foundations, structured data, analytics, testing, and launch planning. The written scope identifies every included deliverable.

Does Elettro work with an existing marketing team?

Yes. Elettro works as a production partner, project lead, or specialist layer. Responsibilities, approvals, file access, and delivery ownership are defined at the start.

Does Elettro work outside South Florida?

Yes. Elettro is based in Davie, Florida and supports clients across the United States and internationally through remote production workflows.

How does Elettro price projects?

Projects are scoped around goals, pages, systems, content, integrations, production requirements, and review cycles. Elettro provides a written estimate after discovery.

Does Elettro provide ongoing support?

Yes. Ongoing support is available for approved updates, reporting, content expansion, technical maintenance, campaign assets, and optimization work.

What information should I provide before requesting a proposal?

Share your current website, main business goal, target audience, required services, timing, available content, technical constraints, and the action you want visitors to take.
