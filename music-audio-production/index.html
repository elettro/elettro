Direct Answer

Why should audio rights be planned early?

The intended platforms, territories, duration, paid advertising use, distribution, ownership, and third-party source material affect what audio is appropriate. Rights questions identified early prevent weak substitutions and delivery delays.

Elettro records the source and approved use of project audio as part of the production handoff.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers before the first project conversation

Each answer appears as visible page content and matching structured data.

Does Elettro produce music in different genres?

Yes. Direction begins with references, intended audience, emotional goal, pacing, instrumentation, and final use rather than a single house genre.

Does Elettro mix audio for video?

Yes. Video audio work includes dialogue balance, music, effects, transitions, noise control, and delivery loudness based on the platform.

Are stems included?

Stem delivery depends on the production agreement and source rights. Required stems, alternate versions, instrumentals, and file formats should be identified before production.

Does Elettro help with digital releases?

Yes. Support is available for file preparation, metadata organization, artwork coordination, platform links, promotional assets, and release planning.

Who owns the music?

Ownership, licenses, credits, publishing, master rights, and permitted use must be defined in the written agreement for each project.

Does Elettro edit podcasts or spoken-word audio?

Yes. Spoken-word work includes structural editing, cleanup, level balance, intros, outros, music integration, and delivery preparation.
