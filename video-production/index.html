Direct Answer

Why should formats be planned before editing?

A wide website video and a vertical social clip use different compositions, pacing, text placement, and viewer behavior. Planning formats early protects important subjects and text while reducing weak mechanical crops later.

Elettro treats each required ratio as a designed output connected to the same core message.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers before the first project conversation

Each answer appears as visible page content and matching structured data.

Does Elettro film original footage?

Production scope depends on location, crew, equipment, talent, and creative requirements. Elettro also edits client footage, licensed material, animation, photography, and approved AI-assisted visuals.

Does Elettro produce vertical video?

Yes. Vertical video is designed for the 9:16 frame with mobile safe areas, readable text, appropriate pacing, and a composition built for the vertical view.

Does Elettro add captions?

Yes. Captioned delivery is available. Captions support accessibility, silent viewing, comprehension, and reuse across platforms.

How are revisions handled?

The project scope defines review stages, decision owners, included revision rounds, feedback format, and the point when structural changes affect cost or timing.

Does Elettro create video for websites?

Yes. Website video is prepared around page purpose, loading behavior, dimensions, poster frames, mobile fallback, compression, and accessibility.

Who provides music rights?

Every project identifies the source and permitted use of music, footage, photography, voice, logos, and other third-party assets. Clients must approve and hold the rights required for supplied material.
