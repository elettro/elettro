Where it goes

If it can be printed, it can be scanned.

  • Album and single coversPlay the video, open the song, sell the vinyl.
  • Merch and shirtsA print on a shirt starts the clip and links to the shop.
  • Posters and flyersOne poster, one code, a ticket link that you can change after printing.
  • Packaging and menusShow the product in 3D, the story behind it, or today's special.
  • Signs and storefrontsOpen a tour, a booking page or a coupon from the street.
