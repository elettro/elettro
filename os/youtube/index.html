One scope. One job: publish the video the owner approved.

Elettro OS asks for youtube.upload so a channel owner can plan a video, approve it, and have the app upload it on schedule. It is the narrowest scope that can create a video.

There is no scope between read-only and full management that allows an upload except youtube.upload.

What the app does with it

  • Uploads only videos the owner approved in the app
  • Sets title, description, tags, language, privacy, AI label, paid promotion
  • Stores tokens encrypted in AWS Secrets Manager
  • Lets the owner disconnect at any time

What it never does

  • Approve and post videos without your approval for each video
  • Edit or delete existing videos
  • Share YouTube data with third parties

Reviewer path at os.elettro.com

  1. Sign in with the reviewer account supplied in the email. No phone or card checks.
  2. Open Connections. The only brand is Google Review, with one YouTube card.
  3. Click Connect YouTube, pick any channel you own and approve the youtube.upload consent. If Google shows an unverified-app notice, choose Continue.
  4. Open Review and select a planned YouTube video, or click Plan a week, wait 1-2 minutes, refresh and open the week.
  5. Review each asset for approval. If all is good, click Approve.
  6. At the next 15-minute tick the video uploads and the status changes to Published with a link.
The app is already In Production. The demo was recorded in the same project, so no unverified scope was deployed to other users.
