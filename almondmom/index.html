Our story · A family joke

It all started with Aunt Cindy.

For Dean Palermo and his family, the Almond Mom concept started as a running joke. His kids and his sister Cindy's kids would jokingly call Cindy the quintessential almond mom.

Cindy has always taken a proactive, sometimes hilariously enthusiastic approach to what her kids eat and drink. She cares deeply about their health, pays close attention to what goes into their food, and takes her role as a mom seriously.

The kids found the whole thing funny. Dean saw something worth celebrating: a mom who cares a little extra, a family with a sense of humor, and an idea for a brand.

ALMOND MOM is a playful tribute to Cindy and all the moms who show their love through thoughtful everyday choices. It's about enjoying life, caring for your family and laughing at yourself along the way. No food guilt. No perfection required.

Dean Palermo · Brand creator
