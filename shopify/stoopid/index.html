Slightly Stoopid Merch Template Pilot

Twenty sample posts built from what the official Slightly Stoopid store (slightlystoopid.shop.redstarmerch.com) publishes: product photos, titles, variants and descriptions. Each post is drawn live in CSS from those photos, with the Facebook and Instagram/TikTok captions underneath. Pick the templates to keep; the keepers then rotate across the full catalog.
