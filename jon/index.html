Elettro AR.
Point your phone at it.

Augmented reality for bands and brands: aim a phone at an image, a poster or a screen and the content comes alive. Try it yourself in five steps.

Open AR on your phone → Open on your computer →
  1. Sit in front of your computer. You need both screens.
  2. On your phone's browser, go to ar.elettro.com.
  3. On your computer, open elettro.com/ar.
  4. Point your phone at every trigger on the computer screen. See what's possible.
  5. Think bigger. Picture an enterprise version of this, implemented and managed across many bands and brands and all of their assets.
