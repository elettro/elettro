How it works

Shoot. Scan. Upload. Make it.

The phones don't need to be synced, connected or even pointed the same way. Sound lines the clips up afterwards.

1

We open a session

One QR code for your event, on a screen, a sign, a ticket or a runner packet. It only works for your event and expires when it ends.

2

Everyone films

Band members, fans, family, crew. Fixed angles and roaming phones. Partial shots count: twenty seconds of a solo still makes the cut.

3

Scan and upload

No app, no login. Pick the song or the spot, pick the clip, send. Full-quality files, straight from the camera roll.

4

Make it

You describe the look in a sentence and choose the outputs. We cut the multi-cam video, the shorts and the stills, and you approve before anything posts.

Questions

Before you ask.

Do people need to install an app?

No. They scan the QR code and a page opens in their phone's browser. No account, no login: they type their name once and pick their clip.

Should people record in the page or in their camera?

In their normal camera app. It gives the best quality, and the camera roll keeps a safety copy. They upload when the song or moment is over.

Does the quality hold up?

We take the original files, not compressed copies, so it's as good as the phone that shot it. We start at 1080p for speed and can finish in 4K.

How fast do we get the video?

It depends on the event and how much footage comes in. We agree on turnaround before the day; for a band session the first cuts can come the same day.

Who can upload, and who sees the clips?

Only people with your event's QR code can upload, and it stops working when the session ends. Clips go to your private library; nothing is published without your approval. For public events, uploaders agree before sending.

What does it cost?

MultiCam is a white-glove service right now, quoted per event based on the number of people, the footage and what you want made. Tell us about yours and we'll come back with a plan.
