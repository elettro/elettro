Direct Answer

What makes a business website effective?

An effective website tells the right visitor what the company does, who it serves, why it is credible, and what action to take. It loads reliably, works across devices, presents proof near decisions, and gives search systems enough crawlable context to understand each page.

Visual quality matters. Clear structure, useful content, performance, and measurement determine whether the design produces business value.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers before the first project conversation

Each answer appears as visible page content and matching structured data.

Does Elettro build new websites or redesign existing websites?

Both. Elettro builds new sites and replaces outdated sites. Redesign projects include an inventory of valuable existing pages, content, links, search signals, and required redirects.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Yes. Responsive behavior is part of the core build. Navigation, forms, media, text, buttons, and layout are reviewed across common viewport sizes.

Will the website include SEO?

Every website receives foundational technical and on-page SEO. Broader keyword research, content expansion, local SEO, authority development, and ongoing reporting are scoped through the SEO and AIO service.

Who owns the finished website?

Ownership and access are defined in the project agreement. Elettro provides the approved production files and account handoff described in the scope after final payment.

What platform does Elettro use?

The platform depends on the project. Elettro works with static websites, GitHub-based publishing, WordPress, Shopify, and custom systems when each platform fits the business need.

What content is needed before the project begins?

Useful starting materials include the current site, logo files, brand references, service descriptions, staff information, photos, videos, testimonials approved for publication, legal text, and conversion goals.

How long does a website project take?

Timing depends on page count, content readiness, integrations, review cycles, and technical complexity. The written scope sets milestones and approval dates before production begins.

Does Elettro provide hosting?

Hosting options depend on the platform and traffic requirements. The proposal identifies the hosting owner, monthly cost, publishing workflow, backup responsibility, and required accounts.
