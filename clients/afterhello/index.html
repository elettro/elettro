This audit evaluates afterhello.com — the website of After Hello, a professional matchmaking agency founded by Ann Parnes (former Assistant District Attorney) and Maria Murray — across five core dimensions of modern search performance.

After Hello has a genuinely compelling brand story and real press momentum: featured in People, Business Insider, NBC, ABC News, AP, and NPR, an active 925-follower Instagram presence, a viral founder narrative (lawyer-to-matchmaker career change), 11-city service footprint across the U.S., and Matchmakers Alliance membership. The site is built on Webflow — a strong technical foundation — with a clean, well-organized service and location architecture already in place.

This is one of the better-built sites in this audit series. The opportunity here isn't a platform rebuild — it's closing the schema and AIO gap on top of an already-solid foundation, which means the path to a high score is faster and lower-risk than most of the sites we've reviewed.

01  —  Platform Analysis

Is It WordPress? What's the Stack?

Good News — This Is Webflow, Not WordPress

The site is built on Webflow — confirmed by the meta-generator: Webflow tag and CDN asset paths (cdn.prod.website-files.com). Webflow is a strong choice for a service business: it produces clean semantic HTML, doesn't carry WordPress's plugin bloat or Odoo's ERP overhead, and gives designers full control over markup. This is one of the better technical foundations in this audit series — the gaps identified below are optimization opportunities, not platform-level rebuilds.

Ceiling Assessment — Why This Is a 90+ Site Already in Progress

After Hello is the rare audit in this series where the foundation is already strong — Webflow, clean architecture, 11 city pages, genuinely excellent FAQ writing, real press, named credentialed founders. This isn't a rebuild. It's a schema and polish engagement, which means the timeline to 90+ is measurably shorter than sites requiring platform migration.

The AIO jump from 38 → 92 (+54 points) is the standout opportunity, and it's almost entirely a schema-implementation task rather than new content creation — the content already exists and is genuinely good. FAQPage schema on the existing FAQ content, LocalBusiness schema across all 11 city pages, Person schema for Ann and Maria with sameAs links to their press coverage, Service schema for the four offerings, and Review/AggregateRating schema for the six testimonials. Once live, AI systems asked "best matchmaking service in Denver" or "how is matchmaking different from dating apps" have a fully structured, citable source directly from afterhello.com.

Two quick fixes carry outsized weight here: the broken phone number (footer text doesn't match the tel: link — actively costing leads on mobile) and the unfinished FAQ answer ("Yes and here is the link to see them" with no link). Both are five-minute fixes with immediate trust and conversion impact, independent of any larger schema work.

Given the strength of the existing foundation, 91+ is achievable within 30–45 days — the fastest turnaround of any site in this audit series, because the work is additive rather than corrective.

04  —  Recommended Actions

Top Priority Fixes

01

Fix the Phone Number Mismatch — Today

The footer displays +1-786-309-2258 as the visible phone text, but the underlying tel: link routes to a completely different number (504-233-4006). Anyone tapping to call from mobile — the primary device for most matchmaking inquiries — reaches the wrong number. This is a five-minute Webflow fix and should happen before anything else on this list.

Fix Today
02

Complete the Unfinished FAQ Answer

The final FAQ question, "Are there any success stories or testimonials available from previous clients?", currently answers "Yes and here is the link to see them" with no actual link inserted. This is live on the site today. Either link to the testimonials section, a dedicated success stories page, or remove the placeholder language and write a complete answer.

Fix Today
03

Implement FAQPage Schema on Existing FAQ Content

The FAQ content on this site is genuinely excellent — well-reasoned, specific, and written in a tone that AI systems favor for direct citation. Adding FAQPage schema to the 9 existing questions (after fixing #2) is the single highest-leverage action in this entire audit: it requires zero new content, just structured markup around content that already exists. This alone could meaningfully shift how AI systems answer "how does matchmaking work" and "how is this different from dating apps" queries.

AIO +25 pts
04

Add LocalBusiness Schema to All 11 City Pages

Each of the 11 location pages (Atlanta, Austin, Chicago, Dallas, Denver, Houston, Las Vegas, Miami, Minneapolis, Phoenix, Scottsdale) should carry its own LocalBusiness schema with city-specific geo coordinates, areaServed, and a unique meta description referencing local market context. This transforms 11 already-built pages from generic location landing pages into structured local entities that AI systems can match to "matchmaking services in [city]" queries with confidence.

Local SEO + AIO
05

Add Person Schema for Ann Parnes & Maria Murray

Ann's former-ADA-to-matchmaker story is press-verified (People, Business Insider) and is exactly the kind of founder narrative AI systems surface when asked "who founded After Hello" or "best matchmakers with unique backgrounds." Person schema with jobTitle, worksFor, sameAs links to LinkedIn and the press coverage transforms this from a compelling story buried in team bios into a structured, citable entity.

AIO + Founder Authority
06

Add Service + Review Schema for the Four Core Offerings

Matchmaking, Date Coaching, Dating Profile Management, and The Inner Circle VIP Membership are each distinct, well-described offerings that deserve independent Service schema (name, description, provider, areaServed). The six named testimonials should carry Review schema with AggregateRating rolling up to the Organization level — giving AI systems and Google rich results verified, structured social proof rather than plain testimonial text.

AIO + Rich Results
