03 / Audit findings

What is working.
What we would improve.

Our recommendations prioritize verifiable changes that improve the site's usefulness and make Plasencia's facts easier for search and AI systems to interpret.

01Existing strength

Excellent source material

Collection and terroir pages explain provenance, product distinctions and historical context. Preserve their editorial depth.

02Existing strength

Thoughtful accessible patterns

Skip navigation, semantic headings, image descriptions and an accessibility statement are positive signs on the current site.

03Priority opportunity

Schema and entity graph

Audit and enrich appropriate Organization, WebSite, BreadcrumbList and product/collection structured data. Test eligibility and align names, locations, brand and heritage references.

04Priority opportunity

Legacy URL governance

Map historical WordPress addresses and uploads, preserve valuable backlinks, apply sensible 301 redirects and remove obsolete or duplicate search entry points where appropriate.

05Priority opportunity

Answer-focused editorial hubs

Publish concise verified answers to brand, farming, production, sustainability and distribution questions with strong citations and clear page ownership.

06Priority opportunity

Mobile and locator QA

Measure LCP, INP and CLS before migration. Improve retailer/distributor discovery and review generic external “Website” links in the distributor directory.

04 / Elettro roadmap

A focused 90-day plan.

A disciplined migration and content plan matters more than merely changing the name of the underlying technology.

Days 01–30 / Diagnose
01

Benchmark everything.

Crawl the full site. Run desktop/mobile Lighthouse and field metrics. Audit indexability, metadata, canonical tags, redirects, JSON-LD, accessibility and current hosting.

Days 31–60 / Build
02

Engineer the optimized experience.

Where evidence supports a rebuild, ship semantic static-first HTML, lean CSS/JavaScript, next-gen images, fast delivery, clean URL mapping, structured content and preserved age-verification controls.

Days 61–90 / Validate
03

Prove the gains.

QA in production, submit updated sitemaps, monitor search indexing and crawl errors, benchmark mobile/desktop, verify schema, and assess AI citation visibility against a baseline.
