This audit evaluates sonalasense.com — the corporate website of SonALAsense, Inc., a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company pioneering Sonodynamic Therapy (SDT) for cancer treatment — across five core dimensions of modern search performance.

SonALAsense carries extraordinary real-world authority: first company in the world to advance SDT into clinical trials, a $46MM ARPA-H award from the U.S. Department of Health & Human Services, $20M+ raised from accredited biotech investors, active Phase 1/2 clinical trials for glioblastoma and DIPG, and a new Mayo Clinic / NCI collaboration trial beginning August 2025. Founder Dr. Stuart Marcus brings over 30 years of ALA pharmacology experience and multiple FDA-approved drug-device combination products to his credit.

The website is built on Wix.com — a platform with known structural SEO limitations — and is missing the schema markup and AIO optimization that a company of this scientific and institutional standing urgently needs. The gap between SonALAsense's clinical authority and its digital discoverability is large, consequential, and entirely fixable.

01  —  Platform Analysis

Is It WordPress? What's the Stack?

Wix Platform Warning — Structural SEO Limitations

The site is built on Wix.com — confirmed by the meta-generator tag and all asset URLs (static.wixstatic.com). Wix is a capable website builder for general use, but it carries well-documented limitations for SEO and AIO: JavaScript-heavy rendering that crawlers struggle with, limited schema markup control, restricted URL structure flexibility, bloated page weight, and no direct access to the HTML head for custom structured data injection. The page's meta-format-detection and meta-skype_toolbar boilerplate tags are Wix noise with no SEO value. For a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company competing for YMYL (Your Money or Your Life) trust signals, Wix is a platform ceiling — not a foundation.

Ceiling Assessment — Can SonALAsense Hit 90+?

Yes — and SonALAsense is one of the strongest candidates for a 90+ overall score in this entire audit series. The reason is straightforward: the company holds government-verified, first-mover, federally funded scientific credentials — exactly the kind of institutional authority that Google and AI systems treat as maximum-weight trust signals in YMYL categories. The $46MM ARPA-H award from U.S. HHS, the ClinicalTrials.gov registrations, the Mayo Clinic partnership, and the Barrow/Ivy Brain Tumor Center collaborations are all independently verifiable from the highest-authority sources on the web. None of that authority is currently structured in a way that search engines or AI systems can efficiently extract and amplify.

The AIO jump from 30 → 92 is the headline number and is the most impactful score gain in this audit. A complete medical schema package — MedicalOrganization, Drug (SONALA-001), MedicalStudy for each clinical trial, MedicalCondition for glioblastoma/DIPG/blood cancers, Person for Dr. Marcus and the leadership team — transforms this site from a blank entity to the most authoritative cited source for sonodynamic therapy on the web. AI systems asked "What is sonodynamic therapy?" or "Is there a clinical trial for glioblastoma?" will cite sonalasense.com rather than third-party medical databases.

Moving from the current Wix platform to our standard clean static HTML architecture eliminates the JavaScript rendering overhead, gives full control over the HTML head for schema injection, eliminates the bloated image carousel loop, and removes all Wix boilerplate meta noise. The rebuild also updates the © 2022 footer, fixes the tap-to-call disable, corrects the CTA links pointing to wrong pages, and establishes proper heading hierarchy — all mechanical fixes that collectively move the needle on technical and on-page scores simultaneously.

The 90+ ceiling is achievable within 60–90 days of the rebuild going live. Sustaining it over 92–95 requires ongoing clinical milestone press releases formatted as NewsArticle schema, peer-reviewed publication citations marked up with ScholarlyArticle schema, and a patient FAQ section that answers the questions patients and caregivers are already asking AI systems. That content strategy compounds over time — each new trial result, each new partnership announcement, each new publication becomes a citable, structured, AI-surfaceable authority signal.

04  —  Recommended Actions

Top Priority Fixes

01

Migrate from Wix to Clean Static HTML Architecture

Wix is the foundational platform constraint on every score in this audit. A migration to our standard static HTML/CSS/JS build eliminates JavaScript rendering overhead for crawlers, gives full control of the HTML head for schema injection, removes all Wix boilerplate noise, resolves the infinite image carousel loop bloating the HTML, and delivers significantly faster Core Web Vitals scores. For a clinical-stage company that relies on institutional credibility, the platform itself must match the science. This is the single action that unlocks everything else.

Foundation — Fix First
02

Implement Full Medical Schema Markup Package

This is the highest-ROI action for AI search visibility. The schema package for SonALAsense: MedicalOrganization (name, url, description, medicalSpecialty: Oncology, address: Berkeley CA); Drug schema for SONALA-001 (activeIngredient: aminolevulinic acid, drugClass: sonodynamic therapy agent, mechanismOfAction); MedicalStudy for Phase 0/1 rHGG and Phase 1/2 DIPG (identifier linking to ClinicalTrials.gov NCT numbers); MedicalCondition for glioblastoma, DIPG, leukemia, myeloma; Person schema for Dr. Stuart Marcus with credentials, publications, and sameAs links. Once live, AI systems can build a complete, verified entity model from the official site — not from third-party databases.

AIO +62 pts
03

Rewrite Homepage Title Tag + H1 to Lead with the ARPA-H Award

The $46MM ARPA-H award is the most credibility-dense fact about this company and it is completely absent from the title tag and H1. Proposed title: "SonALAsense — Sonodynamic Therapy Pioneer | $46MM ARPA-H Award | Clinical Trials for Brain & Blood Cancers." Proposed H1 (semantic, not just animated text): "Pioneering Sonodynamic Therapy — The First SDT Company in Clinical Trials." The animated "Soundly Defeating Cancer" headline can remain as brand copy below the H1, but a semantic heading must carry keyword weight for Google and AI parsing.
