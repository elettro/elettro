Ceiling Assessment — Can FINTECH.TV Terminal Hit 90+?

Yes — and this is one of the most dramatic score-gap opportunities in this entire audit series. The terminal launched with an F overall score not because the product or the brand is weak, but because the robots.txt crawl block is functioning as an SEO kill switch on one of the most technically significant financial media platforms launched in 2026.

The core fix — building a public-facing, SEO-optimized product landing page separate from the authenticated dashboard, with proper schema and the crawl block limited to the /app/ or /dashboard/ paths only — immediately unlocks every other optimization. You cannot write meta descriptions, implement schema, or build keyword rankings on a URL that returns a crawl block to every search engine on earth.

Once the crawl architecture is resolved, the brand's existing authority accelerates everything else. NYSE studio presence, ADX partnership, and Morningstar/Gulf Business press coverage are exactly the trust signals Google and AI systems use to fast-track new content to high rankings. FINTECH.TV doesn't need to build authority from scratch — it needs to make the authority it already has accessible to crawlers.

With a proper static public landing page, full schema package, and the robots.txt scoped correctly, 87–91 is achievable within 60–90 days of Google re-crawling. The Trust & Authority category alone has room to climb to 90+ as the Terminal accumulates user reviews, press coverage, and third-party citations post-launch.

04  —  Recommended Actions

Top Priority Fixes

01

Fix Robots.txt — Scope the Crawl Block to /app/ Only

The current robots.txt disallows all crawlers from terminal.fintech.tv entirely. The correct approach is to allow crawling of the public-facing marketing/landing pages while blocking only the authenticated dashboard paths (e.g. /app/, /dashboard/, /portfolio/). This single change unlocks every other optimization in this list. Without it, nothing else matters — Google and all AI systems see a door with a "Do Not Enter" sign.

Critical — Fix First
02

Build a Public-Facing Terminal Landing Page

Create a fully crawlable, SEO-optimized marketing page at terminal.fintech.tv/features (or /about) that exists independently of the authenticated app. This page targets: "financial markets terminal for retail investors," "Bloomberg alternative 2026," "real-time market data platform," and "customizable financial dashboard." It becomes the indexed entry point that drives signups, organic traffic, and AI citation — the authenticated dashboard remains gated behind login.

High Impact
03

Implement Full Schema Markup Package

WebApplication schema for the Terminal (name, description, applicationCategory: "FinanceApplication", featureList of the 70+ widgets, offers/pricing). Organization schema for FINTECH Media Group with sameAs links to NYSE presence, ADX partnership, LinkedIn, and press coverage. Person schema for Vince Molinari as CEO. NewsMediaOrganization schema connecting cms.fintech.tv editorial content to the parent entity. This makes FINTECH.TV Terminal a fully citable entity in AI search responses.

AIO +50 pts
04

Create Widget & Feature Pages (70+ Widgets = 70+ SEO Assets)

Each of the Terminal's 70+ widget categories — U.S. equities, global equities, digital assets, commodities, prediction markets, live broadcast, social audio — is a distinct keyword cluster. Dedicated pages for each major category function as individual organic search landing pages. "Cryptocurrency portfolio widget," "real-time stock dashboard," and "prediction markets tracker" are all rankable terms with commercial intent that no competitor owns cleanly.

High Impact
05

Add FAQPage Schema — Own the AI Answer Layer for Terminal Queries

AI systems are already being asked: "What is the FINTECH.TV Terminal?", "Is FINTECH.TV Terminal free?", "How does FINTECH.TV Terminal compare to Bloomberg?", "What data does FINTECH.TV Terminal cover?" A dedicated FAQ page with FAQPage schema structured to answer these questions directly makes FINTECH.TV Terminal the authoritative cited source for its own category — rather than letting press releases and third-party comparisons define the narrative.

AIO +20 pts
06

YMYL Compliance — Financial Disclaimer & E-E-A-T Architecture

The FINTECH.TV Terminal is YMYL (Your Money or Your Life) content — Google applies heightened scrutiny to financial platforms. Proper E-E-A-T architecture requires: a named editorial team page with credentials, a clear "Not Financial Advice" disclaimer structured with LegalNotice schema, regulatory/compliance information, and named author attribution on all cms.fintech.tv articles. Without this, Google's quality raters will suppress the site's ranking potential regardless of technical optimization.
