The opportunity

A singular destination deserves a singular digital world.

Bal Harbour Shops already brings together exceptional luxury houses, a beautiful open-air setting, personal shopping, dining, e-commerce and a sophisticated editorial magazine. The opportunity is to connect these assets into one more intuitive, visually commanding guest experience.

Elettro proposes a unified creative and publishing system. The site becomes a curated destination. The property becomes digitally navigable. Magazine advertising becomes interactive. Every participating boutique gains a new path from inspiration to visit.
