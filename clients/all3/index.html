Flagship finding: claim fragmentation

The site says 10%. Its own press release and the press say 30%.

The homepage, nav and /less-cost page promise a guaranteed minimum 10% cost reduction. The WaVe-X press release on the same domain and third-party coverage of the seed round (RoboticsTomorrow) cite "up to 30%". Both can be true (floor vs ceiling), but nothing on the site reconciles them. AI assistants synthesize from all of these sources and will quote whichever number they hit first, or blend them.

SourceCost claimFraming
all3.com homepage + nav10%"10% less cost"
all3.com/less-cost≥10%Guaranteed at contract stage
all3.com/news (WaVe-X release)up to 30%Model ceiling
RoboticsTomorrow (seed round coverage)up to 30%Model ceiling

Fix: one canonical claim sentence used everywhere, e.g. "guaranteed 10% minimum, up to 30%", added to /less-cost, the FAQ, Organization schema description and the press-release boilerplate.

Findings

Critical

Homepage title is just "All3"

No category, no keyword, no meta description. The brand's most-linked page tells Google nothing about robotic or timber construction. Core pages (/less-cost, /mantis, /faq) follow the same pattern: bare titles, no descriptions.

Critical

FAQ is AIO gold with no confirmed markup

18 long-form Q&As on CLT fire safety, timber supply, factories and pricing model. This is exactly what AI assistants lift. FAQPage schema unconfirmed and the page has no meta description.

High

Social previews are text-only

Twitter card is "summary" with no image on every page checked; og:image not detected. LinkedIn is the only social channel, so every shared link lands as a bare title.

High

Alt text leaks and placeholders

English FAQ images carry German alt text ("Holz als Baustoff", "Installationszeit…", "Sigel"). Others read "Image description" and "Gallery slide". A visible "Enter some text..." placeholder renders on news articles.

High

Mixed-case, inconsistent news URLs

/news/WaVe-X-invests-in-all3, /news/all3-awarded-grant-from-Innovate-UK, /news/All3-mantis-revealed. Case-sensitive paths risk duplicate URLs and broken inbound links.

Medium

Offices invisible on-site

Zug, Berlin, London and Belgrade appear only in press boilerplate. No Organization/address signals in the footer, which weakens entity confidence for "construction robotics Germany" style queries.

Medium

Heavy media on first paint

1920px autoplay hero video, gallery images served at 3840px width, a client-loaded 360° viewer ("Loading…"), plus GTM and a Builder.io tracking pixel. Performance score is estimated; confirm with PageSpeed Insights.

Medium

EN/DE toggle, hreflang unverified

German market is the first commercial market. If hreflang pairs are missing, DE pages compete with EN pages instead of serving German searchers. unconfirmed

Strength

News articles are done right

Descriptive titles, meta descriptions, og:type article and published_time. Proof the CMS can do it; the core pages just weren't configured.

Strength

Real third-party authority

$25M seed (RTP Global), WaVe-X / WALTER GROUP investment, Innovate UK grant, Automate.org feature, a running podcast series. Authority is not the bottleneck.

What the Elettro rebuild changes

  1. Claim alignmentOne canonical cost / time / CO₂ statement across site, schema and press boilerplate.
  2. Metadata on every core pageKeyword-led titles and descriptions for home, the three benefit pages, Mantis, factory, RTA, FAQ, About. og:image and large-image cards throughout.
  3. Schema layerOrganization (with all four offices, founders, funding sameAs), Product for Mantis / RTA / Robofactory, FAQPage, NewsArticle, VideoObject for The Constructivist.
  4. AIO packagingAnswer-first summaries on each benefit page, llms.txt, a spec sheet page for Mantis (payload, reach, doorway fit) that assistants can quote.
  5. HygieneLowercase URL redirects, EN/DE alt-text split, placeholder removal, hreflang pairs, responsive image sizes, poster-first hero video.
