Flagship finding

High · Entity / AIO

"Seaspice" is not a unique name anymore

At least three other businesses trade as SeaSpice or Sea Spice. The closest is a Gusto Group rooftop concept in San Antonio, described in recent coverage as French Riviera-inspired seafood with a raw bar, caviar service and late-night DJs. That is nearly word for word how Seaspice Miami is positioned. Coverage calls it the group's first SeaSpice location, and I saw no sign of any link to the Miami restaurant.

Others: a Sea Spice spice boutique in St. Barts, and a SeaSpice Seafood & Thai in Fort Lauderdale (2019 article). Search engines and AI assistants will blend these unless the Miami entity is spelled out.

Why it matters: the homepage title is "Welcome To Seaspice" with no city, no "Miami River" and no cuisine. Nothing on the page disambiguates the entity for a crawler or an LLM, and no structured data was confirmed that would do it either.

Elettro fix: city and neighborhood in titles and H1s, one canonical entity record with sameAs links to OpenTable, Google, Instagram and press, and an About page that states who, where, since when.

What's already working

  • Menus are real HTML text, fully crawlable, with named dishes and ingredients
  • Heavy third-party proof: about 3,900 OpenTable reviews at roughly 4.4–4.5, Time Out and Tripexpert coverage, GMCVB listing, Brides and Diners' Choice badges
  • Canonical tags on every page fetched, mobile viewport set, skip-to-content link present
  • Events page lists room-by-room capacities with a Tripleseat inquiry form
  • Active blog with recent posts, plus Press, Press Kit and Careers pages
  • Address and phone in the header on every page
  • Third-party ratings differ by source (OpenTable 4.3–4.7 across regional snapshots; aggregators show Google about 4.2 and Tripadvisor about 4.0), so reputation messaging needs one verified source
  • Most menu items carry no prices outside Sunset Hour and wine
  • Hero carousels and large galleries on every page add weight
  • Platform templates limit how much title, schema and URL structure can change without a rebuild

How the Elettro build gets to 91

  • Rewrite titles, H1s and meta descriptions per page with Miami River and cuisine context
  • Split /menus/ into dedicated brunch, happy hour, wine, Miami Spice and bottle service pages
  • Build Restaurant, Menu, Event and FAQ schema tied to one entity record with sameAs links
  • Write an About page with named people, history and AIR / Omakase / yacht relationships
  • Add an FAQ covering dock access, parking, dress code, dietary needs and private events
  • Replace auto-generated alt text with descriptive, dish and room-specific alts
