Flagship finding

The doctor page ranks as a CV, not as an entity.

Excellent raw credentials (DC, NUHS 1981, Nagoya acupuncture certification, ACSM 1996, FIU/Barry/UM lectures) sit in unstructured prose. The title tag is "Meet Dr Paul Canali – Evolutionary Healing Institute": no specialty, no city, no condition terms. Search engines and AI assistants get a name and a list of events, not a clearly defined practitioner with a practice, location and service set.

Findings

High

Title tag has no keywords or geography

Name + brand only. Nothing signals trauma therapy, chronic pain, chiropractic, or South Miami. Homepage title is stronger (condition terms) but the doctor page, the entity page for the founder, is not.

High

Empty alt text on content images

The doctor headshot, Omega certificate and workshop images render with blank alt text. Only the logo is described.

High

NAP fragmentation across the web

Site: 6601 SW 80 St, Ste 200A, South Miami, 305.667.8174. NPI registry: 7800 S Red Rd, same phone. One directory places the institute in Coral Gables; another lists it under 7800 SW 57th Ave. The 200A suite is also listed for a separate practice (Dr. Charlene Canali) with a different phone.

Medium

Core media hosted on a third-party domain

The Dr. Ash radio interview MP3 and the 2021 seminar thumbnail load from jimfazioib.com. Off-domain dependency, and the audio carries no transcript on the page.

Medium

Stale signals

Footer reads ©2024; the featured seminar is dated July 2021; lecture list stops at 2018. An older domain (evolutionaryhealinginstitute.com) is still cited by third parties.

Medium

Unclaimed third-party profiles

A Birdeye profile for Dr. Canali is unclaimed (4.8 average, 19 reviews). One review there disputes the clinical claims, and a third-party blog also questions his approach. Both rank in branded search; the site has no owned counter-surface such as a reviews page with markup.

Medium

Absolute-claim language on a YMYL topic

The bio refers to "cures" for a vast number of diseases. Beyond compliance risk, vague medical-outcome claims without citations weaken E-E-A-T for health content. Disclaimer is present, which helps.

Medium

Heading hierarchy jumps

H1 followed by H5 section heads, with H2–H4 skipped. Credentials, lectures and workshops should be H2/H3 blocks.

Strength

Real depth and a ownable brand

Unified Therapy™, Enteric Brain Technique™, Somatic Fusion™ are distinctive coined entities. Site map is broad (ACE study, interviews, workshops, practitioners). Canonical and max-image-preview are set.

Unconfirmed

Schema, meta description, Open Graph

Automated fetch strips script tags and returned no meta description or OG tags. Treat as unconfirmed until checked in view-source or the Rich Results Test. No Person/Physician/MedicalBusiness markup is detectable from rendered content.
