Key Findings

No confirmable structured dataHigh
No JSON-LD (Product, Organization, BreadcrumbList) surfaced on the homepage. Wix does inject some default schema, but coverage on product/collection pages is typically thin and needs manual verification via Google Rich Results Test before any claims are finalized.
No llms.txt / AI-crawler guidanceHigh
No answer-engine optimization layer present. Product descriptions read as marketing copy rather than structured, extractable facts (ingredients, use-case, benefit) that LLM-based answer engines can cite directly.
Local citation inconsistencyMedium
A third-party directory listing under this domain and address shows mismatched business category data (unrelated service type), suggesting NAP/entity data isn't tightly controlled across citations — a signal issue for local and AI entity recognition.
Thin FAQ / answer-format contentMedium
No FAQ blocks detected on the homepage. Adding structured Q&A content (ingredient safety, usage frequency, hair type suitability) is one of the highest-leverage AIO moves for a personal care brand.
Solid brand story & trust signalsLow
"Our Story," community-giving messaging, and multiple customer testimonials are present — good raw material for E-E-A-T once restructured with schema and clearer authorship/sourcing signals.
Homepage title tag is reasonableLow
"Shop True Blend Essentials | Natural Hair & Skin Care for All" includes relevant category keywords. Product and collection page titles were not individually audited in this pass.

Marketing Footprint Detected

Instagram — activeActive
@trueblendessentials is live and posting (e.g. scalp-health content). Primary social channel based on visibility. Not confirmed as linked from the homepage header in the fetch — worth checking footer/nav social links are present and correct.
Facebook — activeActive
Facebook Page is live under "True Blend Essentials | Winston-Salem NC," confirming a local market anchor. Good for local social proof, but reinforces the NAP-consistency finding above — worth auditing all directory/social listings for matching address and category data.
Content marketing exists — but it's off-platformFragmented
Blog content (e.g. "Itchy Scalp After Braids") is being published on a separate Blogspot domain rather than the Wix site's own blog. This is a significant AIO/SEO leak: topical authority, backlinks, and answer-engine-citable content are accruing to a domain the brand doesn't fully control and that isn't linked into trueblendessentials.com's entity graph.
No confirmed email capture / retention flowGap
No popup, discount-for-signup, or newsletter capture was visible on the homepage fetch. For a consumables/repeat-purchase category like hair & skin care, this is a meaningful revenue gap independent of SEO — worth flagging as a broader marketing opportunity, not just an audit line item.
Community/mission narrative in useAsset
Homepage messaging ties purchases to community support (food, clothing, essential care). This is a strong, underused trust/E-E-A-T signal — currently living in body copy only, not reinforced with schema (Organization/sameAs) or a dedicated, linkable "Impact" page that social and press could cite.
Methodology note: This audit is based on a live fetch of the homepage plus public search signals; it does not constitute a full crawl. Automated fetching strips inline <script> tags, so structured-data (JSON-LD) presence/absence could not be directly confirmed and should be verified manually via Google's Rich Results Test before using schema findings in client-facing claims. Scores are directional, meant to support a pitch narrative, not a certified technical audit.
