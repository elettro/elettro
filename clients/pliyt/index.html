Important distinction

The score lift does not come from changing platforms by itself.

Framer is capable of producing a visually strong website. The opportunity comes from how much control Elettro gets over every search-facing layer when the site is rebuilt directly in semantic HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

A clean-code rebuild gives the development team direct control over page structure, metadata, internal linking, schema graphs, crawl behavior, image delivery, performance budgets, conversion paths and future AI-search optimizations without working around a visual-builder abstraction.

What the Elettro rebuild changes

Clean code is the foundation. The implementation work is what converts that flexibility into search visibility.

01 / Semantic Foundation

Search-first HTML architecture

One clear H1, logical H2/H3 hierarchy, descriptive landmarks, crawlable text, accessible navigation, deliberate internal links and page-specific topical targeting.

02 / Metadata

Every page gets its own job

Unique titles, descriptions, canonicals, Open Graph data and social metadata replace the current repeated generic “Pliyt” metadata.

03 / Entity Graph

Make Pliyt machine-readable

Organization, Person, FAQPage, Article, BreadcrumbList and relevant product/service schema connect the company, leadership, press and core concepts.

04 / AI Answerability

Give AI systems quotable answers

Concise explanatory copy, FAQs, leadership facts, product definitions and supporting citations make the site easier to retrieve and summarize accurately.

05 / Performance

Control the payload

Purpose-sized assets, responsive images, minimal JavaScript, deferred noncritical behavior and explicit performance budgets protect the premium motion experience.

06 / Ownership

Own the structure

The website becomes a portable codebase rather than a presentation tied to a visual-builder runtime, giving Pliyt more freedom for future integrations and optimization.

Why Pliyt has more upside than a typical site

Great press. Generic entity presentation.

Pliyt secured legitimate national coverage from Forbes, Fox News, MSN and New Atlas. Those stories describe a distinctive privacy-first mobility concept and a leadership team with experience connected to Waymo, Toyota, Volkswagen/Porsche, GE and HELLA.

On the website pages reviewed, the page title is repeatedly “Pliyt” and the meta description is the single word “pliyt”. That means the owned domain is giving search engines less context than the third-party publications covering the company.

The rebuild opportunity is to make pliyt.com the strongest source on the internet for understanding Pliyt.

This is a directional Elettro manual audit based on the live Home, About and News & Media pages, page metadata and headings, site structure, available press/entity signals and implementation inspection. SEO 55 and AIO 38 are summary scores derived from the detailed category findings above. Projected scores are implementation targets, not guarantees of ranking position or traffic. They assume a full clean-code rebuild with unique metadata, technical cleanup, structured data, entity consolidation, answer-ready content, on-domain conversion paths and ongoing validation. Script-delivered analytics or JSON-LD that automated fetching does not expose should be manually verified in a browser before final technical sign-off.
