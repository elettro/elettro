Starting Point

Where the site began.

NadjaAtwal.net already had a strong visual identity, canonical URLs, a sitemap, baseline Person and Podcast schema, and AI-readable files. The opportunity was to turn that promising foundation into a complete authority system with deeper pages, stronger entity signals, and consistent answer-ready structure.

Page footprint

The original site contained 11 indexable pages. The upgraded version contains 16 indexable pages with fuller topic and intent coverage.

Primary gaps

  • Most supporting pages contained fewer than 200 words
  • Incomplete Twitter, author, favicon, and theme metadata
  • No direct-answer format across the supporting pages
  • No structured and visible FAQ system across the site
  • No page-level reviewed dates or complete trust framework
  • Limited page-specific Service, Article, and Contact schema
  • No dedicated About, editorial, or professional disclosure pages
  • No custom 404 page or full sitemap last-modified coverage

Elettro Treatment

What changed.

The treatment aligned technical crawl signals, structured entities, answer-ready content, trust, internal context, media delivery, and repeatable quality control.

01

Technical SEO Completion

Elettro completed titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, indexing directives, social previews, author signals, favicons, theme metadata, and consistent page hierarchy.

02

Entity and Schema Expansion

The site now connects Person, WebSite, Service, PodcastSeries, Article, AboutPage, ContactPage, FAQPage, and BreadcrumbList data through one canonical identity.

03

Answer-Ready Content

Every core page now includes a direct answer, question-led headings, structured FAQs, visible review information, related official resources, and substantive topic depth.

04

Authority Expansion

Elettro added About, Editorial Standards, Professional Disclosure, Executive Positioning, and Speaking Topics pages to strengthen trust and search-intent coverage.

05

AI and Media Infrastructure

Elettro expanded llms.txt and llms-full.txt, added official profile links, created optimized WebP assets, completed the sitemap, and added a branded 404 experience.

06

Repeatable Validation

A repository audit now checks all 16 indexable pages and stops with a failure when a measured SEO, AIO, schema, content, link, image, or infrastructure requirement is missing.
