Flagship Finding

The business doesn't fully agree with itself on what it's called — and no one outside the domain is citing it either way.

Search and AI answer engines resolve identity from consistent naming and third-party corroboration. The Finesse Xperience has neither: the site uses three different name forms across its own pages, and a multi-source search (Google, hospitality trade press, review platforms, business directories) turned up no citations of the business anywhere outside its own domain and social handles — despite About Us copy describing years of hotel and luxury-residence partnerships with specific, quantified outcomes.

What's working, what's costing them

Strengths

Good

Good

Good

Good

Issues

Critical

Zero third-party authority footprint.

Multi-source search (Google, hospitality trade press, review platforms, directories) surfaced no citations of the business anywhere off-domain, despite years of claimed hotel and luxury-residence partnerships.

Critical

No structured data detected.

No FAQPage schema despite a dedicated FAQ page, no LocalBusiness/Organization schema, no Service schema for the six listed categories. Unconfirmed via automated fetch — flagged for manual Rich Results Test verification before citing to the client.

High

Brand/entity name fragmentation across the site itself (see flagship finding above) — fragments how search and AI answer engines resolve the entity.

High

Broken image references sitewide.

Homepage and Services teaser cards render empty image tags with no source, and the homepage photo gallery is built on non-functional javascript: href markup that doesn't resolve.

Medium

Duplicate heading text on the Services page — "Dance & Choreography" and "Games & Activities" headers each repeat 2–3 times, a page-builder templating artifact that muddies heading hierarchy.

Medium

Identical meta description reused verbatim across Home, About, and Services — no page-specific targeting.

Medium

Copyright footer still reads 2018 — no visible content refresh signal in roughly eight years.

Low

Platform ceiling: the page-builder offers no sitemap control and no server-side rendering, capping technical SEO regardless of on-page fixes.
