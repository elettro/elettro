Who is Vince

One résumé, four arenas.

01 — PRACTITIONER

02 — SPEAKER

Keynote Speaker

A globally recognized authority on capital markets, digital assets, and the future of finance — three-time Master of Ceremonies for Fintech Day at Abu Dhabi Finance Week, and a sought-after voice on stages worldwide.
