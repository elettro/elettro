Key Findings

No confirmed structured data (JSON-LD)High
No Organization, ClinicalTrial, NewsArticle, or Person (leadership team) schema was detected in the rendered markup. For a publicly traded clinical-stage biopharma, this is the single biggest gap — schema is what lets Google and AI engines confidently attribute pipeline claims, executive bios, and investor filings to the entity.
No llms.txt / AI-crawler guidanceHigh
No llms.txt file directing AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini) to canonical pipeline, investor, and leadership content. Given the "Helix" name collision with unrelated software/cloud products, entity disambiguation is a real risk for AI-generated answers.
Heading hierarchy used inconsistentlyMedium
Several body paragraphs on the homepage (pipeline descriptions, purpose statement) are marked up as H2 headings rather than paragraph text. This dilutes topical signal for crawlers and makes it harder for AI summarizers to isolate the actual heading structure of the page.
Autoplay MP4 hero backgroundsMedium
The homepage and pipeline section load raw .mp4 video backgrounds directly rather than optimized/streamed video. This is a common Core Web Vitals (LCP) drag on Elementor builds and adds unnecessary payload for mobile visitors, including investors checking the site pre-earnings-call.
Repeated content blocks on homepageMedium
The three pipeline candidate descriptions (L-DOS47, LEUMUNA, GEMCEDA) appear to render twice in the page's document flow — likely a carousel/tab duplication artifact common in Elementor. Worth a manual check, as duplicate on-page text can dilute keyword focus.
Strong metadata fundamentalsLow
Canonical tags, meta description, Open Graph, and Twitter Card metadata are all present and correctly filled — above average for a WordPress/Elementor site. Robots meta correctly allows indexing with large image/video previews.
Genuine authority signals presentLow
Detailed leadership bios with credentials (PhD, MD, CPA), patent coverage dates, FDA Orphan Drug Designation, and press coverage (USA Today, International Business Times) give this site real E-E-A-T substance — it's an underleveraged asset rather than a missing one.
Lazy-load enabled at builder levelLow
Elementor's e_lazyload feature is active site-wide, which is a positive baseline signal for image performance — though it won't offset the video payload issue noted above.

Why AIO Matters More for Helix Than Most Clients

Helix BioPharma sits in a category — clinical-stage oncology, publicly traded — where investors, analysts, and patients increasingly ask AI assistants questions like "what is L-DOS47" or "is Helix BioPharma still developing cancer treatments" before they ever open a search engine. Without schema and an llms.txt entity layer, those answers get assembled from secondary sources (Wikipedia, press aggregators, SEDAR filings) rather than from Helix's own site — and the generic "Helix" name makes disambiguation errors more likely.

An Elettro rebuild closes that gap directly: consistent entity naming, ClinicalTrial and Organization schema, and a structured pipeline/investor content model give AI engines a clean, authoritative source to cite — which is the whole premise of AIO.

Methodology: Scores are directional estimates based on a live fetch of the homepage, meta tag inspection, and technology fingerprinting — not a full crawl of every page on helixbiopharma.com. Automated fetching strips inline <script> tags, so JSON-LD/structured data presence could not be definitively confirmed or ruled out from this pass; a manual check via Google's Rich Results Test is recommended before any structured-data claim is used in a client-facing proposal. Projected scores represent an Elettro full-rebuild target, not a guaranteed outcome — actual results depend on scope, timeline, and ongoing content operations post-launch.
