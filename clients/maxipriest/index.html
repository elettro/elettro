04Key Findings — Current State

The issues driving the current composite score, ranked by impact on discoverability and AI answer-engine readiness.

High Impact
No confirmed entity schema

Without Person/MusicGroup structured data, AI answer engines have no reliable way to extract discography, tour dates, or bio facts directly from the page — the core mechanism behind AIO visibility.

High Impact
Plugin-driven performance drag

Slider Revolution is a known contributor to render-blocking JS/CSS and elevated load times, directly working against Core Web Vitals and mobile page-speed scores.

High Impact
Broken ticket links

Tour date "Tickets" links point to placeholder anchors (#) instead of live ticketing URLs — a direct conversion and trust signal loss for both users and crawlers.

Medium Impact
Unoptimized title & meta description

The homepage title carries no target keywords beyond the artist name, and no meta description was detected — a missed opportunity for both SERP and AI-summary snippet control.

Medium Impact
Text encoding artifacts in bio copy

Apostrophes throughout the biography render as double periods (e.g. "Maxi..s parents"), degrading both readability and the clean text signal AI crawlers rely on for accurate summarization.

Medium Impact
Zero-value image alt text

Collaborator and gallery images use non-descriptive filenames ("11.jpg", "44.jpg") with no alt attributes, leaving image search and accessibility signals entirely untapped.

06Methodology

Scores are directional, manual-audit estimates — not automated crawler or Lighthouse output. Each category score reflects a structured manual review of page source, metadata, on-page copy, and detectable structured data against Elettro's AIO/SEO standard. Projected scores model the expected outcome of a full custom HTML/CSS/JS rebuild applying the tactics listed above; they are not a guarantee and do not account for factors outside on-site control (backlink profile, domain age/authority, off-site brand signals). JSON-LD/structured data findings are based on rendered HTML inspection, which strips script tags — absence of detected schema is flagged as "not confirmed" rather than "confirmed absent," and manual verification via Google Rich Results Test is recommended before final scoping.
