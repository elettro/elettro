Local & Authority
Entity Fragmentation
One Practice, Three Live Domains

The single biggest issue on this account: the same practice is currently split across three separate, independently-live domains, none of which fully cross-link or consolidate authority.

Primary · Live
backinlinefl.com

Current Wix Studio site. Homepage and most service pages, but several core pages sit on unedited default slugs (/blank, /blank-1…/blank-5).

Legacy · Still Live
backinlineonline.com

Older Wix Classic site with different design and duplicate/conflicting copy — including an NFL athlete testimonial (Ronnie Brown) that never made it to the current domain.

Off-Domain Service
regenclinic1.com

Hosts the "Regenerative Cellular Therapy" page and the site's Privacy Policy — the practice's highest-value service line lives entirely off the primary domain.

Findings
Strengths & Issues

SEO Strengths

  • Mobile-responsive viewport meta tag present sitewide
  • Per-page canonical tags correctly implemented
  • Open Graph and Twitter Card metadata present on indexed pages
  • Google Search Console verification already in place
  • Homepage title/description carry primary geo + service keywords ("Chiropractor in Plantation")

SEO Issues

  • Un-renamed URL slugs on core pages — About, Contact, and three service pages live at /blank, /blank-1, /blank-2, /blank-3, /blank-4 High
  • An unfinished page titled "temp page" is live and indexable at /blank-5 Medium
  • Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions footer links both loop back to the homepage instead of real pages Medium
  • "Client Testimonials" nav item routes to the homepage rather than a dedicated, indexable page Medium
  • Entity split across three live domains (see Entity Fragmentation above) High

AIO Strengths

  • Plain-language facts (owners, tenure, location) that answer engines can extract directly
  • Phone number and address stated consistently in on-page copy
  • Active Instagram presence (5,252 followers) as a citable social signal

AIO Issues

  • No JSON-LD structured data confirmed anywhere on site — no Physician, MedicalClinic, Service, or FAQPage schema detected High · Unconfirmed, pending manual Rich Results Test
  • No llms.txt file present Medium
  • An NFL-athlete video testimonial exists only on the abandoned legacy domain, invisible to AI engines crawling the live site High
  • Regenerative medicine — the highest-value service line — is siloed on a separate domain, fragmenting topical authority High
  • Business name varies across the web: "Back In Line Back Chiropractic Center," "Back In Line Integrated Health," "Back in Line Chiropractic Center," "Back In Line Chiropractic Inc" Medium
Content & Trust
Content Depth & Local Authority

Content Depth Gaps

  • Service descriptions run 1–3 short marketing sentences per modality, with no depth on conditions treated, technique, or practitioner credentials
  • Stock photography in active use (filenames like shutterstock_1956381193.jpg) rather than real practice photography — a trust gap in a health-and-wellness (YMYL) category
  • A patient testimonial video is embedded as a broken image link pointing to Vimeo — renders as a missing image for both users and crawlers
  • No structured FAQ content on any service page

Local & Authority Gaps

  • NAP (name/address/phone) inconsistency across directories — at least one third-party listing shows a Deerfield Beach address instead of the actual Plantation location Reported
  • Active Yelp, Facebook, and YouTube profiles exist, but reviews are not surfaced or schema-marked on the owned domain
  • Four+ business-name variants in circulation across medical directories undermine local pack and AI-citation consistency

Methodology
This audit combines live page inspection via automated fetch with supplementary web search for third-party signals (directory listings, review platforms, and technology fingerprinting). Automated fetch strips <script> tag contents, so JSON-LD structured data cannot be confirmed or denied through this method alone — structured-data findings above are marked Unconfirmed and should be verified manually via Google's Rich Results Test or browser View Source before use in final client-facing claims. Site performance figures are directional, based on platform and asset-loading patterns observed rather than a live Lighthouse/PageSpeed Insights or CrUX session — recommend running a live test before finalizing performance claims. NAP inconsistency findings are based on third-party directory listings and should be spot-checked against Google Business Profile before outreach.
