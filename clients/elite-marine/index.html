Findings

What's Holding the Site Back

Directional findings from live-site inspection. Items marked "manual verification recommended" could not be fully confirmed via automated fetch and should be checked with Google's Rich Results Test or browser source view.

Critical — AIO

No llms.txt / AI-crawler guidance

No llms.txt file was found at the domain root. AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini) have no structured entry point to understand the business, meaning Elite Marine is far less likely to be cited or recommended in AI-generated answers about marine A/C services.

Critical — AIO

No structured data detected

No JSON-LD LocalBusiness, Service, or Review schema was surfaced on the homepage. Manual verification recommended via Google's Rich Results Test, since script tags are stripped in automated fetch, but no schema hooks are exposed in the rendered markup either.

Needs Work — On-Page

Generic, duplicated title tag

The homepage title reads "Elite Marine - Elite Marine Air Conditioning" — it repeats the brand name and carries no service or location keywords (e.g. "yacht A/C repair," "Fort Lauderdale"), wasting valuable SERP real estate.

Needs Work — Technical

Elementor page-builder overhead

The site runs on Elementor 4.1.5 with default CSS/JS bundling, which typically ships more markup and render-blocking assets than needed — a common Core Web Vitals drag on WordPress builder sites.

Needs Work — Accessibility/SEO

Missing image alt text

At least one homepage image was found with an empty alt attribute. Missing alt text weakens image search visibility and accessibility compliance, and removes a low-cost keyword opportunity.

Needs Work — Local/Entity

Inconsistent NAP & social entity signals

Business name and social profile handles vary across platforms (Facebook page under "Elite Marine Yachts" vs. a newer profile, differing Instagram handles). This entity inconsistency weakens both traditional local SEO and AI-engine confidence in a single canonical business identity.

Opportunity — Content

Thin site architecture (4 pages)

The site is limited to Home, A/C Services, Water Products, Careers, and Contact. There's no resource/blog content — a significant missed opportunity to build topical authority around marine HVAC, watermakers, and Spot Zero systems that AI engines and Google both reward.

Opportunity — Trust Signals

Strong review base, not surfaced on-site

Third-party listings show a 4.7-star rating across 80+ reviews, but none of that is reflected on-site via structured review markup or testimonial content — a ready-made trust and rich-snippet opportunity.

Methodology & Disclaimer

Scores in this report are directional estimates derived from live inspection of elitemarineair.com via automated fetch and manual review — they are not output from a verified crawler diagnostic (e.g. Screaming Frog, Lighthouse CI, or Google Search Console).

  • Automated fetch strips inline <script> tags, so JSON-LD structured data could not be directly confirmed and is flagged as "not found in rendered markup" rather than a certainty — manual verification via Google's Rich Results Test or browser "View Source" is recommended before finalizing scope.
  • Core Web Vitals figures are inferred from platform/stack signals (WordPress + Elementor + multiple third-party embeds), not a live PageSpeed Insights run.
  • Projected scores reflect Elettro's typical outcome range on comparable marine/hospitality rebuilds — not a guarantee.
