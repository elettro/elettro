This audit evaluates vocabulous.us across five core dimensions of modern search performance: Technical SEO, On-Page SEO, Content Quality, AI Search Optimization (AIO), and Trust & Authority Signals.

Vocabulous US has a compelling product story — 7 million downloads, a Best Mobile App Award, press coverage in Forbes, Inc., and HackerNoon, and an active newsletter and social community. The opportunity identified in this audit is significant: almost none of that authority is structured in a way that search engines or AI systems can extract, cite, or amplify. The fix is achievable and the projected score gain is among the largest we have modeled for a client at this stage.

Ceiling Assessment — Can We Hit 90+?

Yes — and Vocabulous US is actually one of the strongest candidates for a 90+ score of any site we've audited this cycle. Here's why: the raw authority already exists in the real world. 7 million downloads, a major award, decade-long operating history, and press in Forbes and Inc. are the inputs AI systems and search engines need to treat a brand as authoritative. The only thing missing is structure — and that's entirely within scope of a rebuild.

The AIO jump from 38 → 91 is the headline number. Once SoftwareApplication schema, Organization schema, AggregateRating schema (pulling from 7M download count and App Store ratings), and FAQPage schema are live, AI systems asked "best free vocabulary app" or "word of the day app with games" have a fully structured, citable, authoritative source to reference. Right now they don't — they surface App Store pages and third-party reviews instead of the official site.

Sustaining 88–92 over time requires one additional commitment: blog articles must be rendered as static HTML, not JS-fetched. The blog content appears to be strong — making it crawlable unlocks compounding keyword authority that no single-page site can achieve. Low-to-mid 90s is the realistic ceiling with a full rebuild plus 90 days of re-indexing.

04  —  Recommended Actions

Top Priority Fixes

01

Implement Full Schema Markup Package

SoftwareApplication schema for Word of the Day (name, description, applicationCategory, operatingSystem, offers, aggregateRating). Organization schema for Vocabulous US (name, url, sameAs, foundingDate, location). Person schema for Dmitry Sukhotski and Vlad Hreben. This is the single highest-ROI action for AIO — transforms the site from invisible to fully citable by AI systems.

AIO +40 pts
02

Rebuild Blog as Static HTML Pages

Blog articles currently load via JavaScript fetch — making them completely invisible to Google, Bing, Perplexity, and every AI crawler. Each article needs its own crawlable URL (/blog/word-of-the-day-tips/, etc.) with proper title, meta description, and canonical. This unlocks the compounding SEO value of all existing and future content.

High Impact
03

Write Homepage Title Tag + Meta Description

"Vocabulous US" as a title tag is the equivalent of naming a storefront "Store." A rebuilt title such as "Word of the Day App — Vocabulary Builder with 7M+ Downloads | Vocabulous US" combined with a 155-character meta description targeting "vocabulary app," "word of the day," and "vocabulary builder" would immediately improve click-through from every search result the site appears in.

High Impact
04

Add OG / Twitter Card Meta + Branded Social Preview Image

No OG tags exist on the site — every link share on LinkedIn, X, Slack, or any AI-sourced preview renders as plain text with no image. A branded 1200×630 preview image featuring the app screenshot and "7M Downloads" would make every social share and AI summary significantly more compelling and credible.

Medium Impact
05

Build Dedicated Pages for App, Press, Awards, and Biblegram

A single-page site cannot rank for multiple distinct keyword clusters. Dedicated pages for /word-of-the-day-app/, /press/, /awards/, and /biblegram/ each become independent ranking assets. The Best Mobile App Award (August 2025) and the Biblegram launch (September 2025) are both significant news that deserves permanent, indexed, schema-marked-up web real estate.

High Impact
06

Add FAQPage Schema — Own AI Answer Boxes

Questions like "What is the best free vocabulary app?", "How does Word of the Day work?", "Is Vocabulous US free?", and "What games does the Word of the Day app have?" are actively answered by AI systems — currently sourcing answers from App Store pages and third-party reviews. A dedicated FAQ page with FAQPage schema makes vocabulous.us the authoritative source for these answers.

AIO +15 pts
