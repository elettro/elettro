Good questions

Fence FAQs

Straight answers on permits, pool codes, materials and timing.

Do I need a permit for my fence?

It depends on the city, height and location. We explain what your city requires and help with permit submittals and HOA approval as part of the estimate.

Can you build a fence that meets pool-safety rules?

Yes. We install pool-safety fences and self-closing, self-latching gates. Requirements vary by city, so we review yours with you before we build.

Chain-link, ornamental iron or vinyl: which is right for me?

Chain-link is the most economical and works well on slopes and large areas. Ornamental iron adds security and curb appeal. Vinyl offers privacy with almost no upkeep. We help you weigh them on site.

Do you do repairs as well as new fences?

Yes. We repair and replace gates, posts, panels and sections for residential and commercial customers.

Who marks the underground utilities?

We coordinate 811 utility marking before any digging begins.
