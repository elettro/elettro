High

The homepage title is “Home Page”

The title reads “Carter Fence Co., Inc.: Home Page”. It carries no service and no city, and every inner page leads with the brand name before the keyword. Searchers and AI systems get no signal about what the page offers.

High

Structured data contradicts itself

Two JSON-LD blocks on the homepage both claim the same entity IDs (#org and #localbusiness). They disagree on opening time (08:00 vs 06:30), contact email, sameAs links and service-area cities. When sources conflict, search engines and AI assistants tend to trust none of them.

High

The schema map pin points to the wrong place

The LocalBusiness geo coordinates (34.1096, −119.0817) land roughly 38 miles west of the 13207 Sherman Way shop, in Ventura County. The site’s own llms.txt lists the correct North Hollywood coordinates (34.2011, −118.4256).

High

Placeholder and empty image alt text

Three homepage product cards use the alt text “Text Goes Here”, one image has none, and the logo is just “logo”. Past Projects carries 50 images and 48 of them are labeled “Image 1”, “Image 2” and so on, so Carter’s best proof is invisible to image search.

Medium

Share tags are incomplete and invalid

og:type is set to “Fence Contractor in North Hollywood, California” and twitter:card to the same sentence. Neither is a valid value, and there is no og:image, so links shared on Facebook, text messages or LinkedIn show no preview picture.

Medium

Claims that conflict across the site

The homepage says “over 80 years” in one place and “over 75 years” in another. Posted hours differ from the stated best contact time. The footer and schema list 15 cities while the site has 10 city pages, and Simi Valley appears in the navigation with no page in the sitemap.

Medium

Heavy, aging front-end stack

The homepage loads 52 script tags (28 inline) with 8 third-party hosts, including jQuery, Bootstrap, Owl Carousel and an icon-font CDN. The server reports PHP 7.4.33, which no longer receives security fixes, and the HTTPS header sets Strict-Transport-Security to max-age=0, which switches that protection off.

Medium

Zoom is blocked on phones

The homepage carries two viewport tags, and one sets user-scalable=0 and maximum-scale=1. That prevents pinch-zoom for customers reading specs on a phone and fails accessibility checks. The navigation dropdown parents also link to “#”.

Strength

A genuine head start on AI readiness

Carter already publishes a detailed llms.txt (entity facts, services, sameAs links, knowledge-graph triples), a rich HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema with an offer catalog, FAQ markup, a sitemap, canonical tags, Google Tag Manager and Cloudflare. Few local contractors have this.

Strength

Consistent identity and a real review base

Name, address (13207 Sherman Way, North Hollywood 91605) and main phone (818-983-1515) match across the site, schema and llms.txt. The schema links nine third-party profiles, including Google, Yelp, Angi, BuildZoom and Facebook, and roughly ten five-star Google and Yelp reviews appear on the homepage.

The Elettro build standard

What every Elettro site ships with, and how it moves each score

  • Technical 95: modern hosting and runtime, HSTS on, a single valid viewport tag, a sitemap with true last-modified dates, working navigation links, and a redirect map for every existing URL
  • On-Page 94: “service + city” titles and descriptions on every page with the brand at the end, one H1 per page, descriptive alt text on all images, valid Open Graph and Twitter tags with a real share image
  • Structured Data / AIO 96: one reconciled entity graph (correct geo, one set of hours, one contact email, one sameAs list), Service and FAQPage markup per page, AggregateRating from verified reviews, and an llms.txt kept in step with the site
  • Content 88: a single source of truth for years in business, hours and service area; fence-type × city pages; a captioned, searchable project gallery; answer-style FAQs for permits, HOAs, pool codes and 811
  • Performance 95: no jQuery/Bootstrap/carousel stack, responsive WebP images in place of 600–800 KB JPEGs, lazy loading, and a target LCP under 2.5 s on field data
  • Local & Authority 70: held down on purpose. Reviews, citations and backlinks are off-site work that a website build alone does not deliver. That is where an ongoing AIO retainer moves the number past 90.

Keep

The teal system

A three-stop teal gradient mark with a matching wordmark is rare in a category dominated by black, red and wood-brown. It reads clean, modern and trustworthy, and it is easy to recognize from a truck door.

Tighten

One name, everywhere

The site, schema and llms.txt use “Carter Fence Co., Inc.”, “Carter Fence Co Inc”, “Carter Fence Company” and “Carter Fence”. Pick one primary name, list the rest as alternates, and use it consistently on the Google profile and every directory.

Upgrade

Put the SVG to work

The site serves the logo as a raster file with the alt text “logo”. The vector version scales cleanly for the website, vehicle wraps, yard signs, estimates and invoices, and carries real alt text and structured-data logo markup.

07 / Common questions

Clear answers
upfront.

A proposal should remove uncertainty before a project starts.

Is the score a guarantee of rankings?

No. The current score is a weighted judgment from what is visible on the site, and the Elettro score is an estimate of what a standard build delivers. Rankings, AI recommendations and lead volume depend on competition, reviews, backlinks and time, and are never guaranteed.

Do we have to rebuild the website to fix these issues?

Not for everything. Several findings, such as the conflicting schema, the alt text, the page titles and the share tags, can be corrected on the current site. The full build also solves speed, the aging stack and the estimate experience, and gives Carter one clean template that scales.

Will our rankings drop if the site changes?

A careful migration protects them. Every existing URL is mapped and redirected, titles and content are improved rather than removed, and indexing is monitored after launch.

Do you handle design and development together?

Yes. Elettro produces the site from creative strategy and visual design through front-end development, content integration, technical SEO, launch and ongoing support.

What about reviews, directories and Google Business Profile?

Those are the off-site half of local search. Elettro can build the review-request flow, reconcile Carter’s listings to one name, address and phone, and keep the Google profile posting regularly. That work is scoped separately from the website.

Will we own our website?

Our agreement would specify ownership of your approved custom website deliverables, content, source code and accounts after payment. Licensed third-party components remain subject to their own terms.

Is the linked homepage the final proposed website?

No. It is a working creative study to demonstrate the direction, interactions and page structure. Photos come from Carter’s website, and business facts such as license number, years in business and hours must be confirmed with Carter before launch.
