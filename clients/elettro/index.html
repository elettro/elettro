06 / METHOD

Audit method and limits

The report uses a repeatable source audit. It avoids confusing technical completeness with marketplace results.

What was tested

The automated audit parsed every URL listed in Elettro's sitemap and checked the source files directly.

  • Titles, descriptions, canonicals, and robots directives
  • Open Graph, X cards, favicons, headings, and landmarks
  • Content depth, direct answers, and internal links
  • Image alternative text and explicit dimensions
  • JSON-LD validity and supporting schema coverage
  • FAQ agreement between visible copy and schema
  • Duplicate metadata, duplicate IDs, and broken targets

What requires live data

These signals sit outside the repository score and need production tools or third-party evidence.

  • Google index coverage and selected canonicals
  • Search impressions, clicks, queries, and positions
  • Real-user Core Web Vitals
  • Google Business Profile actions and rankings
  • Review quality, volume, and velocity
  • Backlinks and referring-domain authority
  • Leads, calls, booked projects, and revenue
