What this means

This is not a cosmetic redesign score.

The gain comes from rebuilding how the information is organized and exposed to machines. Clean HTML, CSS and JavaScript give Elettro direct control over every search-facing layer: semantic page structure, page-specific metadata, crawl paths, canonical strategy, internal links, structured data, content hierarchy, asset delivery and future AI-search optimizations.

Sonos Capital does not need to lose its investor portal or regulated offering workflow. The public marketing and education layer can become dramatically stronger while secure investor actions remain integrated with the appropriate portal.

What the Elettro version changes

The rebuild targets the structural weaknesses behind the scores, while preserving the investor-facing business content and compliance requirements.

01 · Architecture

One page becomes a searchable site

Dedicated URLs for the fund overview, manufactured-housing thesis, team, FAQs, investor education and contact content create independently indexable search surfaces.

02 · Entity Layer

Sonos becomes machine-readable

Consistent naming and structured relationships between Sonos Capital, Sonos Capital Fund 2 LLC, Walter Johnson, the team, the investment category and FAQs strengthen AI citability.

03 · On-Page SEO

Every page gets a specific search job

Unique titles, descriptions, headings, internal links and target topics replace a single URL trying to carry every keyword and concept.

04 · AI Search

Answer-ready content structure

Concise definitions, FAQs, entity summaries, structured facts and source relationships improve extraction for AI answer systems and search-generated summaries.

05 · Content Authority

Build topical depth around MHC investing

An insights and education layer gives Sonos searchable authority around manufactured-housing communities, fund strategy and investor questions.

06 · Direct Control

No builder abstraction between strategy and code

Clean HTML, CSS and JavaScript let Elettro tune semantics, delivery, crawl behavior and future optimization at the implementation level.
