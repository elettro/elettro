About Walter Johnson

Walter Johnson is a seasoned Finance Professional and Real Estate Investor/Syndicator with over 16 years of experience across the Real Estate and Banking industries. As Founder and CEO of Sonos Capital, he sources and manages Manufactured Home Parks and Apartment Building opportunities, partnering with accredited and non-accredited investors, banking institutions, and real estate professionals to unlock high-value growth.


Mr. Johnson's career is defined by a rare combination of executive leadership, revenue generation, and relationship-driven business development. He is recognized as a "Top Producer" whose track record spans hundreds of millions in sales and consistent, measurable impact at every organization he has led.

At Freedom Mortgage, Mr. Johnson helped generate $882 million in sales in 2015 and designed a Direct Mail Campaign that drove a 140% increase in revenue. He also spearheaded the development of new offices and cultivated strategic service partner relationships that consistently produced high-converting leads.
