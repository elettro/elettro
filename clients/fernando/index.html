01  —  Platform Analysis

Is It WordPress? What's the Stack?

Elementor Performance Warning

The site runs on WordPress with Elementor 4.1.3 as the page builder. Elementor is a common and capable builder, but it carries a well-documented performance cost: bloated CSS, render-blocking scripts, and Core Web Vitals penalties that can suppress rankings. The version detected (4.1.3) is also outdated relative to the current release, creating potential security exposure. This is the single most important technical finding — it affects every other metric on this site.

Ceiling Assessment — Can We Hit the High 80s?

Yes — 85–88 is solidly achievable within 60–90 days of a proper rebuild, and this site has one of the best raw-material profiles of any personal brand site we have audited. The AIO jump from 18 → 88 is the standout projection, and it's realistic because Fernando's credentials are exactly what AI systems need to build a verified person entity: named press coverage in Forbes, Fast Company, and USA Today; TEDx appearance; NYSE interview; board-level governance roles; a founded company with verifiable public records.

The rebuild strategy that gets him to 85+ is a static HTML site — our standard architecture — with a clean multi-page structure: a homepage, an About/Bio page, a Ventures page (Lightcore Energy + Deutsche Grünstrom Invest), a Speaking/Media page, a Press page, and a Contact page. Each page targets a distinct keyword cluster and gets its own title tag, meta description, and schema markup.

The AIO schema package for this site is particularly valuable: Person schema with `sameAs` links to Lightcore, Deutsche Grünstrom Invest, LinkedIn, and press URLs; Organization schema for each company; Event schema for the TEDx appearances; NewsArticle or Mention schema for each press placement. This transforms the site from invisible to fully machine-readable and citable within days of Google re-crawling.

Reaching 90+ requires sustained content publishing — energy market commentary, expansion updates for Philippines and USA, and ongoing press placement — which is a strategy conversation rather than a build task. The foundation we can deliver immediately sets the ceiling at 88 and positions everything above that as achievable through ongoing content work.

04  —  Recommended Actions

Top Priority Fixes

01

Fix the Staging Email Address — Immediately

The contact email currently displayed is post@fernandogalvaoco-em9wbl0ywg.live-website.com — a WordPress.com staging domain. Every visitor who attempts to reach Fernando via the site is sending mail to a non-production address. This is a broken contact flow on a personal brand site where business inquiries, speaking invitations, and partnership opportunities are the primary conversion event. This is a five-minute fix that cannot wait.

Fix Today
02

Rebuild as Multi-Page Static HTML Site

Replace the single-page Elementor WordPress site with a clean static HTML architecture. Core pages: / (homepage), /about/ (full biography), /ventures/ (Lightcore Energy + Deutsche Grünstrom Invest), /speaking/ (TEDx, Demographics Day, NYSE), /press/ (all six publications + future placements), /contact/. Each page gets a targeted title tag, meta description, H1, and schema block. This is the foundational move that unlocks every score gain on the table above.

Highest Impact
03

Implement Full Schema Markup — Person + Organization + Event

Person schema for Fernando (name, jobTitle "CEO & Founder", worksFor Lightcore Energy, sameAs: LinkedIn, Deutsche Grünstrom Invest, Forbes article URL, Fast Company URL). Organization schema for Lightcore Energy Deutschland GmbH (name, foundingDate: 2018, address: Korschenbroich Germany, url, founder). Event schema for TEDx Convention Manila and Demographics Day Panel. This single package transforms his AI search visibility from an F to an A — AI systems asked about him will finally have a structured, authoritative source to cite.

AIO +70 pts
04

Write Homepage Title Tag + Meta Description Targeting Key Terms

Proposed title: "Fernando Galvao Correa — Green Energy Entrepreneur, TEDx Speaker & CEO of Lightcore Energy Deutschland." Proposed meta description (155 chars): "Fernando Galvao Correa is the founder of Lightcore Energy Deutschland and Deutsche Grünstrom Invest — a global renewable energy leader expanding into the Philippines and USA in 2025." These two fields alone dramatically improve click-through from every Google search result where his name or company appears.

High Impact
05

Add Open Graph + Twitter Card Meta with Branded Social Image

No OG tags exist. Every time his URL is shared on LinkedIn, X, WhatsApp, or any platform — or parsed by an AI preview engine — it shows a blank card with no image, no title, and no description. A professional headshot or brand image (1200×630px) with his name and credentials as OG image, combined with properly written og:title and og:description tags, makes every share a professional brand impression. This is especially important given his active business development in the Philippines and USA.

High Impact

Build a Dedicated Speaking & Media Page

TEDx Convention Manila, Demographics Day Panel, and the NYSE interview with Nadja Atwal are currently mentioned only as image gallery captions — three words each. Each of these appearances deserves a proper page entry with date, event name, topic, location, and a quoted excerpt or video embed. This content (a) builds E-E-A-T credibility for Google's quality raters, (b) makes him discoverable for "TEDx speakers renewable energy Germany" and related queries, and (c) gives AI systems citable event records to include in knowledge responses.
