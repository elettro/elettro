Key findings

What's actually holding the current site back.
!
No meta description

The homepage has no meta description tag, so Google writes its own snippet — usually less compelling than one Elettro would craft to drive clicks.

High impact
!
Generic, duplicated title tag

The homepage title reads "Katchafire – Katchafire" — no keywords, location, or descriptor to help search or AI engines understand what the page is about.

High impact
!
No schema markup

No MusicGroup, Event, or Organization structured data was found. Tour dates and band facts aren't machine-readable, so AI Overviews have nothing clean to cite.

High impact
·
Missing image alt text

Several homepage images, including the featured "Colour Me Life" pre-save graphic, ship with empty alt attributes — a miss for accessibility and image search.

Medium impact
·
Uncompressed hero imagery

Full-size 1024×1024 PNGs are served for promo art with no visible responsive srcset, adding avoidable page weight on mobile connections.

Medium impact
·
Key content trapped in social embeds

News and updates live almost entirely inside the client-rendered Instagram feed — text search engines and AI crawlers largely can't read that content.

Medium impact
·
Thin homepage content depth

Band bio, tour, and press content each live on separate pages with little cross-linking or on-page summary — diluting topical authority on the domain's most-visited URL.

Medium impact
·
No FAQ or citation-ready facts block

There's no concise, structured block of facts (formed, hometown, genre, notable releases) that AI answer engines can lift cleanly into a summary.

Medium impact
