Before you

Is The Sea Lord an adults-only hotel?

Yes. The hotel states guests must be 21 years of age or older.

Does the hotel have direct beach access?

Yes. The property features private beachfront access and an oceanfront heated pool.

How do I check room rates and availability?

Use the Book Your Stay links on this page to reach The Sea Lord's currently linked external reservations platform.

Is the restaurant open year-round?

The hotel describes its restaurant and tiki-bar hours as seasonal. Please confirm current hours with the property.

Is the new wellness center open?

The hotel lists Dominion Wellness Center as coming soon. Existing Sea Lord Spa treatments are already offered subject to availability.
