High-pressure blasting can crack or dislodge tile, which is why we use soft-washing — low-pressure application of a roof-safe cleaning solution that kills algae, moss, and lichen without ever putting mechanical stress on the tile itself.

Those dark streaks are almost always Gloeocapsa magma, a airborne algae that feeds on the limestone filler in roofing tile and shingles. South Florida's heat and humidity make it spread fast, which is why roofs here need cleaning more often than roofs up north.

Most South Florida homes need a soft-wash roof cleaning every 12–24 months, depending on tree coverage, roof pitch, and sun exposure. We'll give you an honest recommendation during your free inspection, not a sales pitch.
