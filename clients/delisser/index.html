Roof Cleaning FAQ

How often should a South Florida tile roof be cleaned?

Many South Florida tile roofs are cleaned every 12 to 24 months, depending on shade, rain exposure, nearby trees, and visible buildup. Delisser Pressure Cleaning can review the roof and suggest a practical timing based on condition.

Is pressure cleaning safe for Spanish tile roofs?

Spanish tile roofs need careful cleaning because tile can be damaged by careless high pressure. Delisser Pressure Cleaning reviews the roof surface and uses a service approach appropriate for the home.

Do you clean driveways and pavers too?

Yes. Delisser Pressure Cleaning also cleans driveways, pavers, patios, walkways, pool decks, and other exterior surfaces for Broward County homes.

How long does roof cleaning take?

Timing depends on roof size, roof pitch, access, weather, and buildup. Many residential roof cleaning jobs can be planned during the estimate process.
