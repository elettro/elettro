What is Elettro?

Elettro Incorporated is a digital production agency based in Davie, Florida. Services include websites, SEO, AIO, AI automation, branding, graphics, video, music, audio, and project production.

Dean Palermo is the founder and executive producer. He brings more than 25 years of experience across digital production, websites, video, social media, AI strategy, SEO, branding, and music.

Where does Elettro work?

Elettro serves South Florida, nationwide, and international clients. Remote production supports discovery, reviews, approvals, file exchange, reporting, and delivery.

What types of clients does Elettro support?

Elettro supports small and midsize businesses, public organizations, healthcare providers, hospitality companies, nonprofits, artists, entertainment brands, ecommerce operations, and technology projects.

How do I request a proposal?

Send the current website, business goal, target audience, required services, timing, available assets, technical requirements, and the action visitors should take. Elettro reviews the information before defining scope.

How are projects priced?

Pricing reflects scope, page count, systems, content, integrations, production requirements, review cycles, travel, licensing, and timing. Elettro provides a written estimate after discovery.

Does Elettro require a deposit?

Payment terms are defined in the written agreement. Production begins after the required agreement, initial payment, access, and starting materials are received.

How are project changes handled?

Requests outside the approved scope are reviewed for impact on cost, timing, dependencies, and completed work. Elettro documents approved changes before adding them to production.

Who provides website content?

Content responsibility depends on the scope. Elettro works with approved client content, restructures existing material, writes scoped copy, and organizes source information. The client approves factual accuracy and required legal or professional claims.

Does Elettro use AI in production?

Yes, where it improves research, iteration, organization, automation, or creative development. Human direction, review, brand control, factual checking, rights, and approval remain part of the process.

Will Elettro guarantee search rankings?

No. Search platforms control rankings. Elettro improves crawlability, relevance, structure, content, evidence, local signals, entity consistency, usability, and measurement.

What is an AIO score?

Elettro's AIO score is an internal readiness benchmark covering crawlable answers, entity clarity, structured data, evidence, page relationships, source signals, local information, and retrieval-friendly content. It is not a score issued by Google.

Will I own the final files?

Ownership, licenses, account access, source files, third-party assets, and handoff are defined in the written agreement. Approved deliverables are transferred according to those terms after final payment.

Does Elettro provide ongoing updates?

Yes. Ongoing work is available for approved content updates, technical maintenance, reporting, SEO and AIO expansion, campaign production, automation improvements, and new pages.

Does Elettro host websites?

Hosting responsibility depends on the platform and scope. The proposal identifies the account owner, publishing method, monthly cost, domain and DNS responsibility, backups, and support boundaries.

How does Elettro measure results?

Measurement is matched to the project. Common events include calls, emails, forms, booking clicks, purchases, directions, downloads, portfolio engagement, organic landing pages, and keyword clusters.

What happens after launch?

Elettro confirms the approved pages, forms, links, analytics, redirects, metadata, schema, sitemaps, and responsive behavior, then monitors the areas included in the post-launch scope.

What is Elettro OS?

Elettro OS is the brand operating system: one login where a brand plans, approves and publishes its content to every social network, keeps its photos, videos and logos in one library, and sees what is working. It replaces the pile of separate apps, spreadsheets, folders and reminders most brands use to stay visible.

What problem does Elettro OS solve?

Staying consistent. Most brands start strong, then posts get skipped, files get lost, every network needs its own login, and nobody can say what is going out next week. Elettro OS keeps the work moving on a schedule, keeps the approvals with you, and keeps everything for a brand in one place.

Who is Elettro OS for?

Businesses, artists, venues, shops and organizations that want steady, on-brand presence without running a social media department. It works for a single brand or for a company with several brands, each with its own library, calendar and rules.

How does it work day to day?

Elettro OS plans a week of posts from your own library and your brand's content themes. You review the week, approve what you like, and swap, edit, pause or remove anything you don't. Approved posts go out on schedule on their own. You stay in control without having to sit at a keyboard.

Does anything go live without my approval?

Your brand's approval settings decide. The weekly review is built so you see the plan before it publishes, and anything you remove or pause does not go out. Reset a week and the unsent posts are cleared so you can start fresh.

Which networks can Elettro OS publish to?

Elettro OS connects a brand's profiles on the major networks, including Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Threads, Pinterest and TikTok, with more added over time. Each brand connects only the profiles it uses, and each network's own rules, such as caption length and image shape, are handled for you.

What is the media library?

A library for every brand with its photos, videos, logos and folders, organized and searchable. Posts are built from the brand's real assets, so what goes out looks like the brand. Nothing in one brand's library appears in another's.

What is the calendar?

A visual calendar of everything scheduled for a brand. You can open any post to see it, change the wording, swap the image or video, pause it, or remove it, so the next few weeks are never a mystery.

Can I post carousels, video and photos?

Yes. Elettro OS handles single photos, video and multi-image carousels, sized and formatted correctly for each network.

Does Elettro OS write the captions?

Captions are drafted to match the brand's voice and content themes and shown for review. You can edit any caption before it goes out.

Can I run more than one brand?

Yes. Each brand has its own profiles, library, calendar, voice and rules, and you switch between them in the same login. A brand can be paused or switched on without affecting the others.

Can my team or my client log in?

Yes. People are given access to only the brands they should see. A client can review and approve their own brand's content without seeing any other brand.

What are content pillars?

Pillars are the themes a brand posts about, for example shows and events, new releases, products, behind the scenes or customer stories. They set the mix of posts in a week, so content stays varied and on message instead of repeating itself.

Does Elettro OS handle Google Business Profile posts?

Business-profile posting follows stricter rules than social posts: every post must tie to a real product or service, fit the profile's photo and wording requirements, and appear inside business hours. Elettro OS is being built to respect those rules automatically.

What reporting do I get?

Elettro OS shows what was published, where and when, and what is scheduled next, with analytics so you can see which networks and which kinds of posts are doing the work. Results are matched to the goals defined for your brand.

What are the add-ons?

Elettro AR, Elettro MultiCam and live dashboards sit beside publishing in the same login. AR turns printed images into scannable experiences. MultiCam collects phone footage from a live event and turns it into finished clips and draft posts for approval. Dashboards show live numbers for the brand.

Do I have to give up control of my accounts?

No. Your profiles stay yours. You connect them with each network's own sign-in screen, and you can disconnect them at any time. Elettro OS only does what the network permits and what you approve.

Is my content private?

Yes. Each brand's files, posts and plans are private to that brand and the people given access. Content is not shared with other brands or used to build other brands' posts.

How do I get started with Elettro OS?

Contact Elettro with your brand, the networks you use and what you want to be known for. Elettro sets up the brand, connects the profiles, organizes the library and the content themes, and walks you through your first weekly review. Pricing is a written estimate after discovery.

Can Elettro run it for me?

Yes. Elettro can operate the system as a managed service, handling planning, production, publishing and reporting, with you approving. Or your team can run it directly with Elettro supporting.

What is Elettro AR?

Elettro AR is app-less augmented reality that runs in the phone's browser. A person scans a QR code, taps Start, allows the camera, and aims the phone at a printed or on-screen image. A video, link page, shop, gallery, form or 3D object then appears on top of that image. Nothing is downloaded and the camera picture stays on the phone.

What is an AR marker?

A marker is the real-world image the phone looks for: an album cover, poster, T-shirt print, product package, menu, sign, business card or logo card. The phone matches what the camera sees against the images registered for that brand.

What is an AR trigger?

A trigger is a marker registered in Elettro OS. It is the image plus the rule that says what happens when the phone recognizes it. When the camera locks onto the trigger, its experience starts and keeps playing until the viewer closes it.

What is the difference between a marker, a trigger and a QR code?

The marker is the printed image you aim at. The trigger is that image registered in Elettro OS with its experience attached. The QR code is the front door: it opens the scanner on the phone. A brand can keep many QR codes and change where each one points later, so a printed code never needs to be reprinted.

What types of markers does Elettro AR recognize?

Elettro AR recognizes flat images with rich detail: album and single covers, posters and flyers, shirt and merch prints, product packaging, menus, signs and storefront graphics, brochures and cards. It matches the registered picture. It does not read faces, identify loose objects or use location today.

What makes a good marker?

A good marker has plenty of detail, contrast and texture across the whole image, and is printed matte and flat. Plain color fields, very small art, blurry prints and strong glare are hard to lock onto. A logo alone has little for the camera to grip, so it works best on a card or label with a pattern around it. Each image is checked for quality before it goes live.

What does an AR trigger launch?

Each trigger can launch one or several elements at once: a video that plays on the artwork, a link page with buttons such as Listen, Buy, Call or Email, a shop card with real products, prices and Buy buttons, a photo gallery slideshow, a 3D object that floats off the image, or a form such as a quote request, contact hub, savings calculator or scratch-and-win coupon.

Can one marker open more than one experience?

Yes. One cover can play a video and show Listen and Buy buttons together. The reverse also works: several different covers can open the same video. Experiences are created once and reused on as many markers as needed.

Can the destination change after something is printed?

Yes. The marker and the QR code stay the same on paper, and the experience behind them is managed in Elettro OS. A poster can point to this week's ticket link, a shirt can start a new clip, and a package can switch to a seasonal offer without reprinting.

Does the viewer need to install an app?

No. Elettro AR runs in the phone's browser on iPhone and Android. The viewer scans the code, taps Start and allows the camera.

Can I try Elettro AR?

Yes. Open elettro.com/ar on a computer, scan the code with a phone, and aim the phone at the sample covers on the screen. The demo shows video, link, shop, gallery, 3D and form experiences for a music brand, a solar shop and a coffee brand.

What is Elettro Remote?

Elettro Remote turns a phone into a wireless controller for a web page. The page shows a QR code, the viewer scans it with the phone camera, and the phone becomes a remote for the screen. There is no app to install and no account to create.

How do I connect my phone to a screen?

Open the experience on the big screen or computer, then scan the QR code shown on it with the phone camera. The phone opens a controller page and pairs with the screen directly. The controller shows Connecting while it pairs and changes when the screen is ready.

What can the phone control?

The controller has direction arrows with swipe, an OK button, Back, Play, Menu, Home, and volume up and down. Tilt mode moves on-screen backgrounds as the phone moves, and games add a joystick and action buttons. The screen responds in real time.

What kinds of experiences use Elettro Remote?

Interactive kiosks, retail and mall displays, hotel lobbies, restaurants, event screens and live venues. Examples include browsing brands and campaign videos from a phone, ticket and lineup screens, and multiplayer games where many guests join from their own phones, such as trivia and the Reef.

Can more than one phone join the same screen?

Yes, for multiplayer experiences. Each guest scans the same QR code and plays on their own phone while the shared screen shows the game, scores and leaderboard.

Can the viewer take something away from the screen?

Yes. The Send to my phone button passes the selected item, such as a link, offer, ticket page or video, to the viewer's own phone so it is saved after they walk away.

Does Elettro Remote work on a regular computer?

Yes. Any modern browser can show the screen side. Keyboard arrows, Enter and Esc work for testing, and the demo includes a test option on the computer. The phone only needs a camera to scan the code and a current browser.

Why use a phone instead of a touchscreen?

Public touchscreens are shared, hard to keep clean and expensive to maintain. Guests already carry a phone, so the screen can be a normal display or TV and the phone does the touching. It also works on screens that are out of reach, such as large video walls.

Can I try Elettro Remote?

Yes. Open elettro.com/remote on a computer or TV, scan the QR code with a phone, and control the demo from your hand.

What is a custom dynamic dashboard?

A custom dynamic dashboard is a web page built around your business that updates itself from live data. Instead of a static report, it shows moving numbers, progress, rankings and status as they change, on a phone, a laptop or a wall display, with no refresh button.

What can a dashboard show?

Anything your systems can report: sales, orders, leads, scans, posts published, connection status, campaign results, inventory, ticket sales, event attendance, energy use, or progress toward a goal. Elettro can combine several sources on one screen.

Where does the data come from?

Common sources include Elettro OS, Shopify and other store platforms, analytics, social and ad platforms, forms and booking tools, spreadsheets, APIs and live counters. The proposal lists the sources, how often each one updates and who owns each account.

Is it really live?

It can be. Counters and status boards can stream updates the moment something happens. Reports that depend on slower platform data refresh on a schedule, and the dashboard shows when it was last updated.

Can a dashboard be designed like a game or a show instead of a spreadsheet?

Yes. Dashboards are designed to be looked at and talked about. Elettro has built progress as a race with animated racers, a connection matrix where plugs seat into sockets, and live counters that tick as people scan. The goal is a screen your team and clients actually want to watch.

Can I control a dashboard from my phone?

Yes. Elettro Remote pairs a phone with the dashboard so you can scroll, jump sections and present it on a big screen without touching the computer.

Can a dashboard track Elettro AR and Elettro Remote activity?

Yes. The Scan Counter shows each time a phone locks onto an AR marker, and the Remote Counter ticks when a phone joins an experience. They count activity only and never record names, addresses or faces.

Who can see my dashboard?

Access is defined in the proposal. A dashboard can be public for a campaign, private behind a login for a team, or shown only on a screen in your location. Sensitive data stays behind your accounts.

Do I own the dashboard?

Ownership, hosting, data accounts and handoff are defined in the written agreement, like every Elettro project. Elettro can also keep it running, add sources and refine it over time.

How are custom dashboards priced and timed?

Pricing reflects the number of data sources, how custom the visual design is, live versus scheduled updates, access rules and display requirements. A focused single-screen dashboard moves faster than a multi-source system. Elettro provides a written estimate after discovery.
